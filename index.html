Why We Exist

We do not exist simply to lend money. We exist to help people access opportunities, build sustainable income, and improve the future of their families.

Through responsible financial support and trusted partnerships, we help hardworking individuals move toward greater financial stability. This is the difference between BLC and an ordinary lending company — our purpose comes before any single product.

About BLC

BeSupport Lending Corporation (BLC) is a Philippine-based lending company focused on responsible financial and livelihood support. We create opportunities for hardworking individuals and families by providing transparent, structured, and responsible financial solutions.

Our goal is not only to provide access to financing, but also to support sustainable income opportunities and long-term financial stability.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about who we are and what we do.

What does BLC do?

We provide responsible financial and livelihood support. Our purpose is to help hardworking people access opportunities, build sustainable income, and improve the future of their families — not simply to lend money.

Is BLC only a payday or salary loan company?

No. Salary-linked lending may be one program we offer, but it does not define BLC. Our broader purpose is to create opportunities, support livelihoods, and build long-term trust across a range of responsible financial solutions.

Who can BLC help?

We aim to support hardworking individuals, families, employees, drivers and self-employed workers, employers, and business partners — as well as organisations working toward financial inclusion.

Which services are available right now?

Only services that are legally approved and operational are offered today. Other solutions are being developed carefully and are clearly labelled as in development, under review, or coming soon.

Are the rates and terms shown final?

Any figures shown on this site are illustrative examples only. Actual rates, fees, terms, and repayment methods are subject to final management and compliance approval, and nothing is published as a confirmed offer without that approval.

Does BLC finance drivers or vehicles?

We are studying livelihood-support programs that may include support for drivers and vehicle acquisition. These are still under review and are not yet available. We will only present them once the business model, legal structure, and procedures are confirmed.
